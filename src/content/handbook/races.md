# Races

What races are available is up to the GM, and depends on the campaign setting. Each race lists minimums and maximums for some attributes. If your attributes do not fall within the specified range you may not choose that race. Those scores can be exceeded later if the attribute is improved.

## Human

Humans are the most populous race, and are the most varied. Any rolled combination of attributes is allowed. Humans are also large and hale, in general, compared to the other races.

{{raceEnd}}

Dwarves are a proud and ancient folk, and their numbers are dwindling. Secure in their mountain strongholds, most dwarves have little sympathy for the troubles of the fools living on the open surface.

Halflings are not widely known, preferring the peace of their hidden home. Warm, friendly and so often underestimated, Halflings occasionally venture forth in search of fortune, or adventure.

{{raceStats:halfling}}

{{raceEnd}}

## Elves

Elves do not die of age, and have watched the world diminish, as they see it, through the centuries. Elven children are rare, and cherished. They are rarely allowed to leave the core elven homes until they are at least 100 years old. 

Elves receive a very thorough, wide ranging education in their first hundred years of life, before they are considered adults. They learn things slowly, carefully, and deeply. When you can live for 1,000 years or more, it’s worth the time. As a consequence, Elvish players get +10 to their free base in all Trades, and can speak and read one extra language for free. They also have -2 skill points/level, but pay 10% lower maintenance costs.
Elves have advantage on all Awareness checks.

## Lizardfolk

LizardFolk originated in the great southern jungle, but now have spread into surprising places. They are relentless and implacable foes.

{{raceStats:lizardfolk}}

Cold Blooded: Immune to fear, charm, agony and many potions. 2 actions/turn. In conditions above 85*f (30c) they gain a third action. In conditions below 40*f (5c) they have only 1 action. Clothing is only effective in reducing wind chill during the first few minutes, as there is very little body heat to insulate.

{{raceEnd}}

{{raceStart:elderling left}}

## Elderling


Elderlings are tall, willowy and scaled in Brilliantly colored patterns. Altered forever by their bond with vain and fickle dragons, they remain a proud and resourceful people. 

{{raceStats:elderling}}



Conditioned: The dragons like obedient servants. Elderlings have disadvantage on all defensive Willpower checks.
Designed creature: pesky concerns like longevity and reliable organ function are less important to the dragons than magical ability and Elegance.

{{raceEnd}}

## Undead Human

Zombies are generally mindless creatures, but there is a lot of magic out there and strange things happen.

{{raceStats:undeadHuman}}

Rotting: Undead do not heal normally. In addition, they lose 1 hit point from each body section every sunrise. They can restore hit points by spending 1 action and 1 mana per hit point. All fire damage they take is doubled. Fire Damage, and damage below 0 in any body action costs 4 actions and 4 mana per hit point. Severed limbs can be reattached but not regrown. If the Torso is ever reduced to 0 hit points, or the Head to -5, the undead dies permanently.
Sleepless: Undead do not need sleep, nor can they. 

{{raceEnd}}
