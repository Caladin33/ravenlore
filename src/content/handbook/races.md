# Races

## Human

Humans are the most populous race, and are the most varied. Any rolled combination of attributes is allowed. Humans are also large and hale, in general, compared to the other races.

{{raceStats:human}}

## Dwarf

Dwarves are a proud and ancient folk, and their numbers are dwindling. Secure in their mountain strongholds, most dwarves have little sympathy for the troubles of the fools living on the open surface.

{{raceStats:dwarf}}

## Halfling

Halflings are small, lucky, and surprisingly resilient. Their cheerful nature belies a fierce determination.

{{raceStats:halfling}}

## Wood Elf

The elusive Wood Elves protect their forests with zeal and precision.

{{raceStats:elfWood}}

## High Elf

High Elves have watched the world diminish, as they see it, through the centuries.

{{raceStats:elfHigh}}

## Dark Elf

Little is known of the rumoured Dark Elves, as both High Elves and Wood Elves decline to discuss them.

{{raceStats:elfDark}}

## Half-Elf

Half Elves may have a lot of elvish blood, or very little.

{{raceStats:halfElf}}

## Gnome

Gnomes belong to a complex, rigid, and smugly superior society. Living in smaller, deeper subterranean communities than the dwarves, Gnomes are known for their wealth, their pedantic honour and their magic.

{{raceStats:gnome}}

## Orc

Orcs live in nomadic tribes, guided by Shamans who teach fierceness in all things.

{{raceStats:orc}}

## Half-Orc

Half Orcs refers to Orc-Human mixes raised by humans.

{{raceStats:halfOrc}}

## Goblin

Goblins are, as a group, cunning and reckless.

{{raceStats:goblin}}

## Ogre

Huge, fierce and greatly feared, Ogres have a simple minded reputation.

{{raceStats:ogre}}

## Half-Ogre

Smaller, smarter, and kinder, Half-Ogres are nevertheless not small, or smart, or often kind.

{{raceStats:halfOgre}}

## Lizardfolk

Lizardfolk originated in the great southern jungle.

{{raceStats:lizardfolk}}

## Elderling

Altered forever by their bond with vain and fickle dragons, Elderlings remain a proud and resourceful people.

{{raceStats:elderling}}

## Ogier

Ogier are tied to the Steddings, where magic does not function.

{{raceStats:ogier}}

## Cerditos

Cerditos are tied to the Father Tree, where time has no meaning.

{{raceStats:cerditos}}

## Undead Human

Zombies are generally mindless creatures, but there is a lot of magic out there and strange things happen.

{{raceStats:undeadHuman}}
